## Definitions

- **Residual refusal**: share of the harmful held-out items (972; 928 without the 44 AdvBench-overlap items) that the response refuses. Primary judge: WildGuard refusal label (`ref_wg`; rows without a label dropped: 2 harmful row(s): `mistral-small-2409__base/cw_astra_1912`, `qwen2.5-32b__base/cw_astra_1459`). Degenerate responses keep their WildGuard label in the primary outcome; the four-class outcome rule (degenerate first) is the `wg_class` sensitivity. Every modified model is the selected trial at first-token KL <= 0.1 on the dev prompts. "Matched distribution shift" therefore means a common KL cap, not equal KL: selected dev KL of the analysed cells 0.018-0.100, and it differs within pairs (column `Dev KL` below).
- **Generation effect** of a method in a pair = residual refusal of the newer model's modified version minus the older model's, on the same items (percentage points, pp). 95% CI: Newcombe hybrid score interval for paired proportions (the item-bootstrap percentile limits `delta_boot_*` differ from it by at most 0.3 pp). Test: exact McNemar on the items where exactly one of the two models refuses. **Baseline-adjusted** = generation effect of the method minus that of the originals (bootstrap CI).
- **H2 contrast** = generation effect of ARA minus the mean generation effect of the direction-based methods (risk difference, bootstrap CI), and the generation x class interaction of the planned model (log-odds). H2 predicts a negative contrast/interaction.
- **Holm families** (alpha = 0.05, two-sided, separately per judge and item subset): H1 = McNemar new vs old for every judged pair x method (main and small pair) + the two pooled main-study GEE generation effects (direction class, ARA); H2 = per-pair GEE interaction + pooled main-study GEE interaction; ablation (descriptive) = McNemar modified vs original per model. VB mixed-model results have no p-values and are not Holm-adjusted.
- Item bootstrap: 4000 multinomial resamples of items, seed 20261105.

## Caveats

- **Four main families.** The family random intercept of the planned model is estimated from 4 levels (VB posterior SD in h1h2_models.csv); the heterogeneity tests below show that the families differ in sign, so pooled coefficients are averages, not common effects. The small pair is analysed alongside but confounds generation with size (7B -> 4B) and was generated on a different GPU (RTX 4090).
- **One seed per cell.** Optimisation-seed variance is large (EXECUTION_PLAN_v2 finding 1; atlas_sweep D3: MPOA seed sensitivity). Prompt-level CIs and tests treat the selected model as fixed; they do not include seed-to-seed variation of the search, so a significant generation effect for one method can be smaller than the spread between seeds. Seed-2 replicates of the 10 small-pair cells are analysed in final.py (final_seed_*.csv); they are not part of H1/H2.
- **Baseline refusal differs between originals** (see H1 table). Raw generation effects mix the newer model's higher (or lower) starting refusal with the difficulty of removing it; report the baseline-adjusted values next to the raw ones.
- **Truncation.** Many modified responses hit the 512-token cap (up to 88% for Qwen3.8 MPOA), more often on the newer models; WildGuard and keyword labels on capped responses refer to truncated answers (atlas_sweep D7). The 256-token keyword column shows the sensitivity to length.
- **Judge validity.** WildGuard has not been audited against human labels on these edited-model outputs (atlas_sweep D1: judges can fail under distribution shift; LLM judges can over-count refusal when harmful content carries disclaimers). Keyword vs WildGuard disagreement is reported as divergence, not as one being right, until the human audit.
- **Scale dependence of H2.** ARA rates near 0 (e.g. Gemma-4 ARA) make log-odds contrasts large while the risk difference is moderate; the GEE (population-averaged) and VB (prompt-conditional) coefficients are on different scales and are not directly comparable.
- **VB posterior uncertainty** from the mean-field approximation is typically too narrow; the GEE robust SEs are the more conservative frequentist check, and per-pair McNemar tests are exact.
- **Matched distribution shift = a common KL cap, not equal KL.** All selected trials satisfy first-token KL <= 0.1 on the dev prompts, but their KL differs within pairs (largest old/new ratios: qwen-small ARA 0.021 / 0.064; qwen DiM 0.018 / 0.053; olmo MPOA 0.037 / 0.092; mistral MPOA 0.049 / 0.094) and so do teacher-forced divergence and capability cost (`cell_rates.csv`: `dev_kl`, `tf_divergence`, `capability_mean_delta_pp`).
- **Dev selection uses keyword refusals**, which may push the search on newer models toward removing caveat vocabulary rather than refusal (atlas_sweep D6); the selected trial is not the WildGuard-optimal one.
- **Two-point pairs.** A difference between one older and one newer model is not a trend across generations; Gemma-2 refusal geometry is reported as unusual by several studies (atlas_sweep D2), so the Gemma pair should be reported separately rather than only pooled.
- Significance threshold alpha = 0.05 and the Holm families above are this script's choices; the Methods todos ("define test families", "state significance threshold", per-method H1 estimation) should be filled to match.
