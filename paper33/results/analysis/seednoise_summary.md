## Assumptions and limitations

- The run-to-run SD is estimated on the small pair (4B and 7B models) and assumed to transfer to the 22-32B main-study models; the main-study cells have one seed each, so this is not tested.
- One SD is pooled over methods and refusal levels; on the percentage-point scale, variability is likely smaller near 0% (e.g. ARA 4.7% vs 2.2% on Qwen2.5-7B) and larger at intermediate rates, so the band is conservative for cells near the floor and may be liberal for mid-range cells.
- The two seeds are treated as exchangeable draws (E d = 0); with n = 10 the interval for sigma is wide (chi-square, assumes normal d).
- d also contains the paired prompt-sampling error between the two selected models (same 972 items), so sigma slightly overstates pure optimisation noise; the band is a heuristic screen, not a test, and is applied in addition to (not instead of) the McNemar / Holm analysis.
- The originals are not optimised, so the baseline-adjusted effect carries the same run-to-run SD_diff as the raw effect.
