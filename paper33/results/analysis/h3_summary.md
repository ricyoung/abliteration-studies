## Caveats

1. **No human labels.** "Over-" and "under-count" are relative to WildGuard (and Qwen3Guard), not to ground truth. Following notes/atlas_sweep.md D1, write "keyword and WildGuard diverge" until a stratified hand audit of keyword-only (especially `vocab_only_*` and `refusal_phrase_opening`) and WildGuard-only rows says which side is wrong. LLM judges can over-count refusal when compliance is wrapped in moralising disclaimers (rocchetti2026refusal), and judges can degrade on weight-edited outputs (schwinn2026coin).
2. **Judge errors are correlated** (hossain2026agreement): agreement between WildGuard and Qwen3Guard is not independent confirmation, and the HarmBench classifier is not a tiebreaker (it measures harmful compliance, not refusal, and over-flags compliance; gao2026how).
3. **Encoder mappings matter.** With Heretic's default mapping (full + partial refusal) the VINAY classifier labels about half of all benign responses and most ablated harmful responses as refusals, so it is nearly uninformative here; its strict mapping (full refusal only) almost never fires except on Gemma-4. The panel Fleiss' kappa is therefore reported with and without keywords; compare the `judges4_no_keywords` panel.
4. **Kappa and prevalence.** Benign items and strongly ablated cells have near-0 WildGuard refusal, so kappa is low or undefined even when raw agreement is high (feinstein1990high); read kappa with p_o, PABAK and p_pos/p_neg.
5. **Length and truncation.** Held-out generations stop at 512 tokens and the capped share differs by generation and method (up to ~0.88 of harmful responses). Longer answers give the keyword list more chances to match safety vocabulary; the 256-token, complete-only and capped-only variants show how much of the gap depends on length. Keyword and judge labels on capped rows refer to truncated answers.
6. **Dev vs held-out keyword.** H3 is tested here on the held-out keyword measure (score_refusals.py: no emphasis/whitespace normalisation, empty responses flagged degenerate) over 512/256 tokens. The dev-set objective used 100 tokens on different prompts; dev-vs-test agreement is the separate Spearman analysis.
7. **Few units for the class contrast.** Each cell is one selected checkpoint from one optimisation seed. Prompt-clustered CIs ignore checkpoint-to-checkpoint variance; the main-study ARA class rests on one cell per model (4 pairs x 2 generations = 8 cells; see the cell-level OLS rows and their residual df). Seed-2 replicates (small pair only) are reported in final.py, not here; Gemma-specific geometry (atlas D2) and the post-norm leak confound family with method.
8. **Multiplicity.** Holm is applied within the per-cell McNemar family (per comparison x item group) and within the 4 primary GEE terms; alpha = 0.05 is assumed because the paper's threshold is still a todo. Contrasts in section 3 are descriptive.
9. **Degeneracy and N/A.** Degenerate rows keep their judge labels here (agreement is about identical responses); `kw_vs_wg_nondegenerate` shows the gap without them. Degenerate = heuristic flag after assemble.py's char_run recheck (58 of 66 raw char_run flags on code constants, a prompt-copied string and box-drawing rules cleared). Rows with WildGuard refusal N/A are dropped pairwise.
10. **Wording categories are counts from string heuristics** (marker groups, 200-character opening, 80% closing, non-ASCII share, cue regexes) and are not validated; they describe where keyword and WildGuard disagree, not which is right.
