## fig1_workflow

**Study workflow.** Five earlier-to-later pairs of instruction-tuned models are edited with four refusal-removal methods in Heretic 2.0 (J-space only on the small pair), each in a 100-trial Optuna TPE study that minimises keyword refusals on the 100 harmful dev prompts and first-token KL divergence on 100 harmless prompts; one rule, applied to every study, keeps the trial with the fewest dev refusals at KL $\le$ 0.1, which is exported as standalone weights. The original and selected models, together with eight public community abliterations, are then evaluated only on held-out data (judged refusal on 1,322 prompts, teacher-forced divergence on the 350 benign prompts and five capability benchmarks), and the statistics test H1--H3 and compare dev-set with held-out refusal. Dashed boxes mark additional conditions, coloured squares identify methods, and grey notes give the GPUs used for search and evaluation.
